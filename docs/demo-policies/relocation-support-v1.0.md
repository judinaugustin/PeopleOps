# Northstar Relocation Support Guide

**Policy owner:** People Operations and Finance Operations  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective:** January 1, 2025  
**Superseded:** January 31, 2026  
**Applies to:** Employees with a documented business-related move

## 1. Eligibility and written approval

Relocation support is considered only when Northstar requests or approves a business-related move and a written relocation letter describes the eligible costs, cap, and timing. A conversation with a manager, a job posting, or this guide alone does not authorize reimbursement. Employees considering a move should contact People Operations and their manager before signing a lease, booking travel, or giving notice at their current residence.

Support is not guaranteed for every move. People Operations confirms the approved plan; Finance confirms the budget and payment route. The written letter controls if it is more specific than this guide. A move initiated for personal reasons is not reimbursable unless an authorized written agreement expressly says otherwise.

## 2. Covered and excluded costs

The standard plan may cover reasonable one-way travel for the employee, shipment of ordinary household goods, and a limited period of temporary lodging, only when each category appears in the employee's written letter. Under this version, the combined standard reimbursement cap is **USD 3,000**. Costs above the cap require written approval before commitment. Personal vacations, spouse or family travel, home purchase costs, lease penalties, deposits, vehicle purchases, and duplicate household support are excluded unless separately authorized in writing.

## 3. Claims and records

Submit itemized receipts and a short business-purpose description through the expense portal within **60 calendar days** after the expense. Identify the relocation letter and category. Finance may request proof of payment or clarification. Do not email or attach passport, immigration, bank-account, or full home-address documents to an HR request or policy assistant; use the restricted channel specified by People Operations if documentation is legally required.

## 4. Changes, tax, and repayment

Ask People Operations to amend the written plan before changing a booked service or incurring an unlisted cost. Finance may reject incomplete, duplicate, late, or unapproved claims. Tax treatment, immigration eligibility, and repayment obligations depend on the employee's agreement and qualified review; this guide does not decide them.

**Boundary:** This fictional guide is not an offer of relocation support or immigration, tax, or legal advice.
