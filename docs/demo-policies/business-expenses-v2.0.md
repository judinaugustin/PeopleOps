# Northstar Business Expense Policy

**Policy owner:** Finance Operations  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Effective:** July 1, 2026  
**Supersedes:** Version 1.0  
**Applies to:** Employees incurring approved business expenses

## 1. Purpose and basic standard

Northstar reimburses reasonable, necessary expenses incurred for an authorized business purpose. The employee is responsible for choosing a cost-conscious option, following approval limits, and submitting a complete claim. Reimbursement is not guaranteed merely because an expense was paid with personal funds or a corporate card.

## 2. Approval before purchase

Obtain written manager approval before committing to a non-routine purchase, software subscription, client event, or travel. A single item expected to cost more than **USD 750** requires both manager and budget-owner approval before purchase. Split purchases must not be used to avoid a threshold. Finance Operations may return a claim that lacks the required approval or charge it to the requesting department after review.

Air, rail, and lodging should be booked through the company travel channel when available. Choose a standard refundable fare when the price difference is reasonable. Business-class travel, companion costs, personal extensions, and upgrades are not reimbursable without an approved written exception.

## 3. Claim and evidence

Submit a claim in the expense portal within **45 calendar days** of the transaction or trip end, whichever is later. Include the date, merchant, amount and currency, business purpose, cost center, and an itemized receipt. For an expense of **USD 50 or less**, an itemized receipt may be omitted if the employee enters a short explanation and certifies that the receipt is unavailable; Finance may request supporting evidence later. A bank statement alone does not establish what was purchased.

For a shared meal, list attendees by name or business role and explain the meeting purpose. Alcohol, personal entertainment, commuting, traffic fines, and expenses for family members are personal unless a written exception is approved in advance. Do not submit the same cost in more than one system.

## 4. Review and payment

The manager confirms business purpose, budget, and approval. Finance checks policy compliance, evidence, coding, and duplicate claims. A returned claim must be corrected in the portal; do not create a second claim for the same transaction. Approved reimbursement follows the normal payroll or accounts-payable cycle. Questions about a tax treatment or local reimbursement right require Finance or a qualified adviser, not the policy assistant.

## 5. Records and exceptions

Keep original receipts until the claim is marked paid or Finance closes a review. Finance retains submitted records under the company retention schedule. An exception must identify the business need, amount, owner, and duration and must be approved by the budget owner and Finance before commitment.

**Boundary:** This fictional policy does not provide tax advice or guarantee reimbursement.
