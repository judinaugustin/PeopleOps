# Northstar Respectful Workplace and Reporting Policy

**Policy owner:** People Operations  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective:** January 1, 2025  
**Superseded:** February 28, 2026  
**Applies to:** Employees, managers, and contingent workers at Northstar

## 1. Standard of conduct

Northstar expects professional conduct in work-related settings, including offices, remote meetings, business travel, and company-sponsored events. Discrimination, harassment, bullying, threats, and retaliation for raising a concern or participating in a review are not acceptable. This policy is a reporting and response process; it is not a legal finding about any person's conduct.

## 2. Raising a concern

An employee may contact their manager, another manager, People Operations, or the confidential ethics channel listed in the company directory. If the concern involves the employee's manager, use another channel. A report may be made by a person who experienced or witnessed conduct. Share dates, locations, people involved, and available records when comfortable, but do not investigate, access another person's account, or circulate private allegations to coworkers.

If there is an immediate threat to personal safety, contact local emergency services or on-site security first, then use a company channel when safe. The policy assistant is not a reporting channel and does not create a confidential report.

## 3. Review process

People Operations acknowledges a report within **five business days** where contact information is available. A trained reviewer assesses the appropriate next step, which may include a private conversation, preservation of records, a neutral fact-finding review, interim workplace arrangements, or referral to a specialist. The reviewer explains the process and expected updates. Northstar limits information sharing to people who need it to respond, consistent with a fair review and applicable requirements; absolute secrecy cannot be promised.

The reviewer considers available information from involved people and records, gives a person responding to an allegation a reasonable opportunity to provide information, and documents the basis for any company action. Interim arrangements are not a conclusion about the report.

## 4. No retaliation and support

Retaliation for making a good-faith report, asking for help, or participating in a review is prohibited. Tell People Operations promptly if retaliation is suspected. Knowingly fabricated information may be addressed through normal processes, but an unsubstantiated report alone is not evidence of bad faith. People Operations can explain support resources and routes for an urgent workplace adjustment.

**Boundary:** This fictional policy does not offer legal advice, guarantee a particular outcome, or replace emergency help.
