# Northstar Learning and Development Reimbursement Policy

**Policy owner:** People Operations and Finance Operations  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Effective:** June 1, 2026  
**Supersedes:** Version 1.0  
**Applies to:** Employees requesting job-related external learning

## 1. Purpose and eligible learning

Northstar may fund learning that builds a skill relevant to an employee's current role or a documented development plan. Eligible items may include a course, professional conference, exam fee, or job-related learning material. Routine internal training and mandatory compliance courses use separate budgets and are not claimed under this policy. A manager should discuss relevance and workload with the employee before approving an application.

## 2. Annual limit and approval

The standard annual reimbursement limit is **USD 1,500 per employee**, measured by the calendar year and subject to available team budget. Obtain written manager approval in the learning portal before enrolling or paying. The approval should name the program, provider, expected dates, total cost, and the business skill it supports. Expenses above the limit require director and Finance approval before commitment. Approval is specific to the listed program and cannot be transferred to another employee.

## 3. Payment and completion

When available, Northstar pays the provider directly. Otherwise, the employee pays and submits an itemized receipt, proof of attendance or completion, and the approval record within **45 calendar days** of completing the program. If the provider cancels or the employee withdraws, notify the manager and Finance promptly and return any refund to the company. Non-completion without an approved reason may make the expense ineligible; People Operations reviews unusual circumstances individually.

## 4. Exclusions and records

Personal-interest courses, degree tuition, commuting, meals not included in an approved conference registration, late fees, and equipment are excluded unless separately approved in writing. A certification renewal may qualify when required for the role and approved in advance. Learning records are retained in the learning system and finance records under their normal retention schedules.

**Boundary:** This fictional policy does not promise an annual benefit or decide tax treatment, employment eligibility, or any repayment obligation.
