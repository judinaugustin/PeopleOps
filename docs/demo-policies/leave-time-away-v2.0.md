# Northstar Leave and Time Away Policy

**Policy owner:** People Operations  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Effective:** April 1, 2026  
**Supersedes:** Version 1.0  
**Applies to:** Northstar employees

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy explains how employees request planned time away, report an absence, and find the holiday calendar assigned to their work location. It describes the Northstar process; it does not replace a written employment agreement or applicable local requirements. Where a local rule or individual agreement provides a greater entitlement, People Operations will route the question for a qualified review.

## 2. Planned vacation and personal time

Submit a request in the PeopleOps portal before making a non-refundable travel commitment. For one to four consecutive scheduled workdays, submit at least **two business days** in advance. For five or more consecutive scheduled workdays, submit at least **10 business days** in advance. These are planning targets, not automatic approval. The manager checks the available balance shown by the HR system, team coverage, and deadlines, then records an approve, decline, or follow-up decision in the portal.

Requests are considered in the order received when coverage is limited. A manager should explain a denial or proposed alternative and should not ask an employee to disclose private medical details to justify ordinary vacation. A request that overlaps a company blackout period requires the department lead's written approval before the manager can approve it. Employees should not assume a request is approved until the portal status changes to approved.

## 3. Illness and unexpected absence

When illness or an urgent personal event prevents work, notify the manager through the normal team absence channel as soon as reasonably possible, preferably before the scheduled start. If the manager is unavailable, notify the designated backup. Record the absence in the HR system when able, normally within two business days after returning. An employee may share only the information needed to explain availability; do not put a diagnosis, treatment record, or medical document in a general HR request or policy assistant.

People Operations handles questions about balances, required records, or a possible workplace adjustment privately. The assistant cannot determine eligibility or approve leave.

## 4. Holidays, balances, and carryover

The official company holiday calendar is assigned by work location and is published in the HR portal each year. Confirm the calendar with People Operations if a transfer or temporary assignment changes the location on file. Balance displays are informational until reconciled by People Operations.

At year end, unused vacation may carry forward up to **48 hours**, subject to the employee's applicable plan. Hours above that cap are not automatically paid or carried forward. Ask People Operations to review a balance discrepancy before planning against it.

## 5. Roles and records

Employees submit requests and update absence records. Managers review coverage and communicate a timely decision. People Operations maintains calendars, interprets plan records, and routes exceptions. The HR system retains request status and decision history; managers should not keep separate medical notes in team files.

**Boundary:** This fictional demo policy is not an actual promise of leave, a legal interpretation, or a substitute for private review by People Operations.
