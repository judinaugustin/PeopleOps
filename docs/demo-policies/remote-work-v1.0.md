# Northstar Remote Work Guide

**Policy owner:** People Operations and Workplace Services  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective:** January 1, 2025  
**Superseded:** April 30, 2026  
**Applies to:** Employees whose role is approved for hybrid or remote work

## 1. Work arrangement

Remote work is a work arrangement, not a change to job duties, work location on record, or employment terms. The employee and manager document the arrangement in the HR portal. Role coverage, customer commitments, secure handling of information, and team collaboration remain required wherever work is performed. The manager may adjust the schedule with reasonable notice when business needs change.

## 2. Team schedule and availability

Each team sets **two anchor days per week** for in-person collaboration. The manager records the selected days and any role-specific coverage hours. Employees should be available during the team's published core collaboration window and should tell the manager promptly if connectivity, caregiving, illness, or another issue affects availability. Calendar status should reflect working hours; it is not a substitute for agreeing on coverage.

Occasional changes to an anchor day may be agreed directly with the manager. A recurring change requires an updated HR portal record. Managers should apply scheduling expectations consistently and may consider documented accessibility or other approved arrangements through the appropriate private process.

## 3. Location and equipment

Work from the approved work location recorded with People Operations. Before working from another country or changing the primary work location for more than **10 consecutive business days**, obtain written review from People Operations and the manager. Do not assume that a short personal trip authorizes work from another location. This guide does not determine tax, immigration, payroll, insurance, or work-authorization eligibility.

Use company-managed devices, approved network access, a private screen, and the normal secure storage and incident-reporting process. Do not use public shared devices for company work. Report a lost device or suspected disclosure to Security promptly. Equipment is returned when requested or when employment ends.

## 4. Workspace and expenses

Employees are responsible for maintaining a safe, reasonably private workspace. Home internet, furniture, utilities, and coworking costs are not reimbursable unless a written accommodation or Finance approval says otherwise. The expense policy governs any approved business cost.

## 5. Review

Managers review an arrangement at least annually and when the role, team coverage, or work location changes. People Operations records approved exceptions and routes cross-border or legal questions for specialist review.

**Boundary:** This fictional guide is not a promise of permanent remote work or authorization to work in a location.
