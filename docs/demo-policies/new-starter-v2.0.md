# Northstar New Starter Guide

**Policy owner:** People Operations  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Effective:** December 1, 2025  
**Supersedes:** Version 1.0  
**Applies to:** New employees and their managers

## 1. Before the first day

People Operations confirms the start date, manager, work location, and required onboarding tasks. The hiring manager submits the standard equipment request after the start date is confirmed. Do not promise an item or special software until Workplace Services approves it. New employees receive account instructions through the approved identity channel; passwords and recovery codes must never be sent by email or included in an HR request.

## 2. First week

During the first **five business days**, the employee should verify sign-in and multifactor authentication, meet the manager and immediate team, review the team handbook, complete assigned security and workplace-safety orientation, and confirm role priorities. The manager schedules an initial check-in and identifies a contact for day-to-day questions. People Operations explains the HR portal, required forms, and where approved policies are stored.

Required training is assigned in the learning system and should be completed by its stated due date. If access or an accommodation prevents completion, tell the manager or People Operations so the task can be routed; do not mark training complete on another person's behalf.

## 3. Equipment and access

Standard equipment requests go through the internal service desk. The employee should use only assigned company-managed devices for company information. Report a damaged, missing, or delayed item through the service desk. A manager may request approved role-specific equipment with a business reason and cost center. **The service desk should acknowledge a new starter's access or equipment ticket within one business day; this is an acknowledgement target, not a delivery guarantee.** Local inventory and delivery dates vary.

## 4. Records and responsibilities

The employee keeps contact details and emergency-contact information current in the restricted HR portal. Managers confirm access and role expectations; People Operations tracks required onboarding steps; Workplace Services handles equipment and account-access issues. Identity documents, bank details, passwords, and medical information must use the designated secure process and must not be uploaded to the policy assistant.

**Boundary:** This fictional guide is a workflow summary. It does not replace a signed offer, local onboarding checklist, or security instruction.
