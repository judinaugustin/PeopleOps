# Privacy and product scope

This portfolio app uses fictional Northstar policies and demo identities. **Do not enter real or sensitive personal information.** Requests and questions are stored in the local database. In live AI mode, retrieved policy passages and the question are sent to the configured OpenAI API. Review current OpenAI API data controls and retention terms before use; this demo does not promise zero retention or legal compliance.

The assistant provides policy information and editable administrative drafts only. A human controls submission and request status. It cannot decide benefits or eligibility, screen candidates, assess performance, recommend discipline, or perform employment actions. It is not legal, tax, medical, or immigration advice.

Conversation deletion/export and configurable retention are not implemented yet. For development data, stop Compose and remove the volume with `docker compose down -v`. Never point this prototype at employer systems or real HR data without authorization and professional privacy/security review.
